Getting around

Trains, bags, airport
  • Glasgow ⇄ Edinburgh: ScotRail via Falkirk High, ~45–50 min, every 15 min. Sort by journey time; buy a return on the day.
  • Edinburgh bags: Waverley left luggage, platform 2, 07:00–23:00, ~£12.50 a bag.
  • Glasgow Central → Queen Street: 5-min walk.
  • Subway: one loop; Buchanan St ⇄ Kelvinhall / Hillhead for the West End.
  • To the airport (Sun): Citylink Air, Buchanan Bus Station → Edinburgh Airport, ~1 h.
  • Edinburgh centre: Waverley, Old Town and New Town are 10–15 min apart on foot.
  • London: contactless on Tube, trains and buses; daily cap.

Good to know

Dark by 16:00, pubs, plugs
  • Sunset ~16:00 London, ~15:45 Scotland: daylight things first.
  • Cold and wet: proper jacket, grippy shoes for cobbles and the Necropolis hill.
  • Pubs: order at the bar, no tipping for drinks. London bills often include 12.5% service.
  • Plugs type G · Emergency 999 or 112 · UK is 1 h behind Copenhagen.
