    Bookings

    Book the sleeper, the Glasgow hotel and both dinners first: it's a busy weekend. Ticks stay on your own phone.

    Getting around

    London

    • Tap in and out with a contactless card or phone on trains, Tube and buses. Daily fares cap automatically.
    • Gatwick → London Bridge: Thameslink, about 30–40 min, same contactless tap.
    • Euston → Glasgow: the Caledonian Sleeper leaves Euston (not King's Cross) at ~23:45 and reaches Glasgow Central ~07:30. Rooms are priced per room, so the twin is shared.
    • Bags on Friday: Euston's left-luggage desk (top of the ramp to platforms 16–18) is open 07:00–23:00; there are no lockers.

    Glasgow

    • Saturday, Glasgow ⇄ Edinburgh: ScotRail Queen Street ⇄ Waverley via Falkirk High is the fast route (~45–50 min), every 15 min on Saturdays. Sort the app by journey time: other routes are much slower. Buy a return on the day.
    • Glasgow Central → Queen Street: about a 5-minute walk.
    • Sunday to the airport: Citylink Air runs direct from Buchanan Bus Station to Edinburgh Airport in about an hour; bags go in the hold.
    • Glasgow's subway is one small loop: Buchanan Street ⇄ Kelvinhall / Hillhead for the West End.

    Edinburgh

    • The centre is walkable: Waverley, the Old Town and the New Town are 10–15 min apart.
    • Bags on Saturday: they stay at the Glasgow hotel, so nothing to carry around Edinburgh.

    Good to know

    • Dark early: sunset is around 16:00 in London and 15:45 in Scotland. Daylight things go first.
    • Weather: late November is cold and often wet. Bring a proper jacket and shoes for the Necropolis hill and Edinburgh's wet cobbles.
    • Pubs: order and pay at the bar; no table service, no tipping for drinks.
    • Restaurants: a 12.5% service charge is often already on the bill in London.
    • Plugs: UK type G (three flat pins).
    • Emergency: 999 or 112.
    • Time: UK is 1 h behind Copenhagen.

    If there's spare time

    London

    • Constantinople to Istanbul, V&A South Kensington, from 7 Nov, £25–27.
    • The Bayeux Tapestry, British Museum, this autumn's big one-off loan.
    • Beyond the Streets, Saatchi Gallery, from 11 Nov: graffiti and street art, £25.
    • Horizon 22, Bishopsgate: free 58th-floor viewing deck, timed booking.
    • St Dunstan in the East and the London Mithraeum, the City: a bombed church garden and a Roman temple, both free.
    • Sir John Soane's Museum, Lincoln's Inn Fields: free, crammed and strange.
    • Wellcome Collection, Euston: free, medicine and the human body.
    • Leake Street Tunnel, Waterloo: legal graffiti tunnel by Waterloo station.
    • Somerset House ice rink, Strand: timed skating sessions.
    • Other workouts: Kobox (boxing HIIT), 1Rebel (Reshape, Ride), Psycle or SoulCycle (cycling).

    Glasgow & Edinburgh

    • Clydeside Distillery, Glasgow: on the river near Finnieston, tour ends with three drams.
    • Holyrood Distillery, Edinburgh: at the foot of Arthur's Seat, ~90 min, £20–25.
    • Port of Leith Distillery: nine-storey vertical distillery with a bar on top.
    • Barnton nuclear bunker tours, Edinburgh: Saturdays and Sundays.
    • Kelvingrove Art Gallery and the West End, Glasgow: Kelvingrove opens 11:00 on Sundays if you swap it for Tennent's.
    • Arthur's Seat, Edinburgh: the hike up from Holyrood is ~1.5 h return; swap it for the Grassmarket lunch and Victoria Street if you'd rather climb.
